HOW TO GET YOUR POLICY JSON
1Pull the compiled policy from your device:
adb pull /sys/fs/selinux/policy ./policy.bin
2Install SETools (apt install setools)
3Run the extractor:
python3 sepolicy_extract.py policy.bin -o policy.json
